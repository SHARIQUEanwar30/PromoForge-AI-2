## What this shows and what it does not

- The parsers and the scoring ran unchanged on three layouts and three genres; every promo passed Python validation.
- For the no-timestamp story, times are ESTIMATED from word counts, every promo is marked low confidence and carries a warning. Real timestamps must be supplied before anyone cuts video from those numbers.
- It does NOT show that the picks are good: these stories are tiny and written by the tool's author. Quality needs human labels (use Keep / Skip in the app, then the `evaluate` command).
